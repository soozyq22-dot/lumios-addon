Install the Lumi App

Get LumiOS as an app on your phone or the front-desk tablet
Step 1 — open LumiOS in your web browser at:
   (staff: ask the manager for the address)

iPhone & iPad

  1. Open the address above in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an ↑ arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap “Add to Home Screen.”
  4. Tap Add — the gold “L” icon appears on your home screen.
  5. Open the app from that icon and sign in.

Android

  1. Open the address above in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top-right).
  3. Tap “Install app” (or “Add to Home screen”).
  4. Confirm — the gold “L” icon appears.
  5. Open the app from that icon and sign in.

Signing in

Tap the Front-desk PIN tab and enter your 4-digit PIN. Demo PINs:

Owner 1234Manager 2345Staff 3456Guest 4567

Note: Manager, Staff & Guest PINs sign in directly. The Owner PIN adds a quick 2-factor code step (a code texted to the owner; in the demo it auto-fills). For everyday front-desk use, sign in as Manager.

(Change these from Admin → Users before going live.)

🔔 Turn on phone alerts (owner/manager): once installed, open the app → Security Panel → tap “Alert my phone” and allow notifications. You’ll get alarms & power outages pushed to you even when the app is closed. (On iPhone you must add to Home Screen first — that’s why this is step 1.)
Note: Inside the salon on Wi-Fi it just works. To use it away from the salon (and for phone alerts), the manager sets up secure remote access once (Tailscale / Cloudflare Tunnel — see the setup guide).
