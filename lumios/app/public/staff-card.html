Lumi Daily Quick-Start

Running the spa with LumiOS — a quick guide for the team
Sign in: on the front-desk tablet, tap your 4-digit PIN (Manager/Staff sign in directly; the Owner PIN adds a quick 2-factor code step). Use the 🎙️ Voice button (top-right) any time instead of tapping.

☀️ Opening up

Tap the Open scene — or say "Good morning Lumi."

Unlocks the front door, brings up the lights, starts calm music, sets a comfy temperature, and turns on the Open sign.

Then run down the Opening Checklist and tick each item — it resets fresh every morning.

🌸 Prepping a treatment room

Tap Treatment Mode and pick the room.

Dims that room to a soft, warm light, lowers the shade for privacy, sets a cozy temperature, and plays spa music — just for that room.

🎵 Setting the vibe

  • One room: open its card, change Music, Light tone or Accent color.
  • Whole areas: use Sound Zones — e.g. upbeat up front, calm in treatment rooms, at the same time.
  • Tap More on a room card for tone, color, presets & shades.
  • Aromatherapy: turn a diffuser on and pick a scent (Lavender, Eucalyptus…) per area.

🔑 Your room

Under Room Access, unlock the treatment room you're assigned to. Every entry is logged automatically.

🔄 Room turnover

On the Room Turnover board, tap a room's status as you go:

  • In use when a client's in the room
  • Clean when they leave (needs turnover)
  • Ready once it's sanitized and set

The front desk sees at a glance which rooms are open.

🧼 Autoclave — every cycle

After each sterilization run, open the Autoclave Sterilization Log and record it: operator, load, cycle, and Pass/Fail. Run the weekly spore test and log it too. This is your compliance record.

🌙 Closing up

  • Goodnight — gentle wind-down (soft lights, shades down, armed Home).
  • Close — locks all doors, lights off, music off, flips the sign to Closed, cameras to night watch.
  • Away / Alarm — full lockdown + motion alerts to the owner.

Say "Lock up for the night" to run Close.

Finish by ticking the Closing Checklist so nothing's missed.

🎙️ Handy voice commands

  • "Good morning Lumi" · "Lock up for the night"
  • "Treatment room 2"
  • "Set reception to 72" · "Dim pedicure to 40%"
  • "Play calm spa everywhere"
  • "Disarm the alarm" · "Show the back camera"
If something looks off — a door won't lock, a light won't respond, or a device shows offline in Equipment — tell the manager. Don't force it; the manager or owner can sort it out.
